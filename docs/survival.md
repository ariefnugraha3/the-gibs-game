## Mode overview

**Survival** (round-based waves — you start with **only a pistol (full ammo pool)**, wave 1 spawns 30 robots [`robotsPerWaveBase` + `Step`·(n−1)]; robots come in three **classes** — C (melee grunt), B & A (ranged shooters, from waves 3 & 6), clearing a wave shows "WAVE CLEARED" + a `shopCountdownSec` countdown, then the between-wave **Field Shop** auto-opens [a clickable mouse-driven menu — buy ammo/health refills, a **Medkit** (held, up to 2; **press 4 to use it INSTANTLY for +70% HP — 2026-07-18, no longer equip-then-hold-left-click**), Heal/Strengthen Monas, the **Radar** (Survival starts without it), and the Shotgun, Assault Rifle & **Grenade Launcher** — **max 3 weapons, buying a 4th prompts which to replace** — plus **per-weapon damage UPGRADES** (2026-07-12; **MERGED 2026-07-17 into ONE card per weapon** — the same card sells the weapon when unowned, then its Lv2/Lv3 upgrades once owned, Vitality-style; each level +25% of base damage; the pistol card appears only while owned [pistol is never sold]), **three SEPARATE ARMOR cards I/II/III** (2026-07-13: blocks 30/50/70% of incoming damage with durability 100/200/300 — any tier buyable directly [skipping allowed]; durability absorbs the FULL base damage of every hit and the armor SHATTERS at 0; re-buying the worn tier while damaged = full repair, an intact worn tier shows "Worn", tiers below the worn one are rejected), and character upgrades **Vitality I/II/III** (three tiers, max HP → 150/200/250, heals the increase) + **Ammo Capacity I/II/III** (three tiers, each +20% of every weapon's base ammo cap except the Grenade Launcher at +15%); LEFT-CLICK a card to buy it / RIGHT-CLICK to undo the last buy, then **Start Next Wave** → "Are you ready?" confirm]; **robots prioritize the Monument** — every robot attacks Monas by default and only aggros onto the player when the player is within `playerAggroMeters` (15 m) of that robot (returning to Monas past it) — and once a robot reaches Monas and starts hitting it, its aggro SHRINKS to `monasLockAggroMeters` (5 m) and a `monasLockChance` (50%) roll may LOCK it fully onto Monas (never chases the player again), `monasHp` 0 does NOT cut straight to game over — it plays a cinematic **MONAS COLLAPSE** (2026-07-13, see the Monas-collapse bullet) then "THE MONUMENT HAS FALLEN"; score = shop currency; from wave 4 on, each wave has a rising chance to trigger a thick grey Monas-fog event)

## Opening cutscene — "The Last Stand at Monas" (2026-07-27)

Picking **Survival** no longer opens a slideshow. The old opener was a DOM presentation — four `.slide` panels of emoji actors (🏃 🤖) in `#cutscene` driven by `initCutscene()` in menu.js, with Next/Skip buttons and dots. **All of it is deleted** (HTML block, ~200 lines of CSS, the menu.js controller); a smoke assert (`SURV INTRO: slideshow DOM lama dihapus`) fails if any of it comes back. In its place, `src/scenes/survival/cutscenes/monasIntro.js` plays a **9-shot in-engine cinematic inside the real Monas park**, built the same way as the campaign intro (`survivalIntroScene` is a non-gameplay scene: every gameplay hook is a no-op, `cinematicActive` freezes control while `updateMode` drives a timer machine, durations from `CFG.survival.intro`).

**The story is unchanged — the four beats of the old slides are a contract** (smoke: `SURV INTRO NARASI 1-4`): a citizen flees the rogue machines → he is not alone, an entire army marches in → he runs to Monas, the last refuge → he stops, turns, and stands his ground ("I WILL FIGHT"). The six narration lines appear as cinematic subtitles (`dom.showCineCaption` → `#cineCaption`, sitting just above the bottom letterbox bar; English, no emoji).

Shots: `city` (near-top-down over the park — scale, with the burning skyline behind) → `flee` (low side tracking, boot dust) → `pursuit` (reverse angle, red eyes closing) → `horde` (**hard CUT** to the fence: the army vaults it in a rolling wave, lands, and marches in ranks — cam shake + a single massed footstep beat) → `refuge` (**CUT** back, behind him, Monas towering ahead) → `arrive` (he decelerates and stops) → `turn` (camera orbits while he turns to face the army) → `stand` (low hero angle) → `settle` (camera eases to exactly `CAM_OFF_DEFAULT` and the fog back to the `outdoor` preset, then hands over to Wave 1).

Details worth knowing before editing it:

- **Camera language.** Each shot has its own azimuth/distance/height (+ per-shot fog) applied by mutating `survivalIntroScene.camOffset` (the existing per-scene hook). Real **cuts** use `setCineFocus(x, z, true)` — the `snap` argument added to renderer.js for this cutscene; without it every angle change is a slow 1.5/s pan. The final `settle` shot exists so the hand-off doesn't *flick*: the tutorial blocker is only 60% black, so a camera snap behind it would be visible, and `SCREEN_UP`/WASD basis must be back to the gameplay azimuth before play starts.
- **The run path is deliberate.** `RUN_PATH` follows the park's diagonal Jalan Silang and the central plaza — exactly the band where `createParkProps` refuses to plant trees — so the hero never clips a trunk even though trees are randomly placed each build (asserted against the real `treeColliders`). It ends at `PLAZA` (0, 120) = survival's spawn point, so the cutscene's last frame is gameplay's first frame. `RUN_U` (in code, not config) assigns each shot its slice of the path so close shots read as a *run* while the wide/cutaway shots don't have to.
- **The robots are cutscene actors, not entities.** They are never pushed into `robots`: no HP, no attacks, no score, untouched by `updateRobots` — so nothing can leak into the wave counters. They reuse `buildRobotMesh` + `animateRobotRig`, are created in `enter()` (still behind the loading screen, so no mid-scene hitch), run through `resolveObstacles` every frame so they slide around trees/fountain instead of through them, and are disposed at the end (`disposeCast`).
- **Music is the one deliberate exception** to "battle music only starts on a bullet hit": the cutscene starts the battle track when the army arrives, ducks it during `settle`, and calls `stopMusic()` on finish — gameplay behavior is byte-identical, since the gameplay scenes still never touch music.
- Auto-play, SKIP button and the "Click to Start the Action" tutorial follow the campaign-intro pattern exactly (auto-play unpauses and hides `#blocker`; the SKIP button only appears on the cutscene's first `updateMode` frame via `cine.live`; `finishSurvivalIntro` enters `survivalScene`, then pauses and shows the tutorial). Dying and restarting does **not** replay it.

## Round system, field shop, Monas objective, scoring & events

**Survival is round-based (overhauled 2026-07-07):** `wave.phase` state machine in `survival/index.js` — `'fighting'` (spawn a FIXED per-wave budget `wave.toSpawn`, capped on-field by `maxConcurrent`) → wave cleared when `toSpawn===0 && robots.length===0` → `'cleared'` (show "WAVE CLEARED" + `shopCountdownSec` countdown in `hudStatus`) → `'shopping'` (auto-`openShop()`; SPACE = `startNextWave()` → `startWave(num+1)`). Weapon inventory: `player.weapons` ordered slots (survival starts `['pistol']`, max 3; `owned` derived via `syncOwnedFromWeapons`); the Shotgun, Assault Rifle & Grenade Launcher are bought in the shop, which appends to a free slot (full loadout) or, when both slots are full, prompts a REPLACE chooser. Field shop (`survival/shop.js`, **clickable mouse-driven MODAL** — game is PAUSED and the pointer released while it's open, the world still renders behind it; auto-opens between waves): **FOUR TABS** (2026-07-15 — `.shopTabs`/`.shopTab`, `itemTab()` classifier + `tabItems`/`visibleTabs`/`firstTabId`, tested via `shopTabDebug()`; **order revised 2026-07-17: General FIRST = the default/opening tab**): **General** (Replenish Ammo/Health, Medkit, Heal Monas, Radar), **Weapons** (ONE merged card per weapon — see the weapon-card note below; the `.rowStart` Buy+Upgrade pairing was removed with the merge), **Armor** (the 3 armor tiers), **Upgrades** (Ammo Capacity + Vitality + Strengthen Monas); empty tabs (e.g. the Monas items in campaign) are hidden. **Every tab uses ONE uniform `.shopGrid` (2 columns, FIXED 42vh height + inner scroll) and the description panel (`.shopDesc`) is a FIXED 104px height (+ inner scroll), so switching tabs NEVER resizes the shop panel** (it's a fixed modal, not a reflowing web page — the armor tab's long descriptions used to grow it). **NO Buy buttons (2026-07-15):** each `.shopCard` (built by `makeCard`) shows the item name + its price/status (`.shopCardPrice`), and **LEFT-CLICK the card buys it immediately, RIGHT-CLICK cancels the purchase you just made** (`shopUndoLast()` — a one-step undo that restores a `snapshotState()` snapshot of all purchase-mutable state [player fields + weapon ammo/levels + Monas hp/max/stage via `getMonasState`/`setMonasState` in index.js] and refunds the score; NOT a general sell-back, only the immediately-previous buy; `refreshOwnedWeapon` is re-run only if the weapon slots changed); hover previews the item in the full-width **description + price** panel (`.shopDesc`) below (hovering only previews; the left-click on the card is the purchase path); bottom-left = Score, bottom-right = **Start Next Wave** button. Items (data-driven `catalog()`; **LEFT-CLICK a card buys it, RIGHT-CLICK undoes the last buy** — `shopPurchase(id)` does the transaction; currency = score): Replenish All Ammo (owned weapons only), Replenish Grenades, **Replenish Health** (`healthCost`; instant full heal to 100%), **Medkit** (`medkitCost`; a HELD medkit — max `CFG.player.maxMedkits` 2, `player.medkits`; press key 4 to use it INSTANTLY for +70% HP — 2026-07-18, no longer equip-then-hold), **Heal Monas** (+25% of Monas max HP), **Strengthen Monas** (raises Monas max HP to the next tier in `CFG.survival.strengthenMonasStages` [3000→4500→6000, last = ceiling] + heals by the increase; scene-local `monasStage`; shows "Maxed" at the ceiling; **the item description deliberately hides the HP numbers** per user request — it only says it reinforces the Monument), **Radar** (`radarCost`; sets `player.hasRadar` → the top-left minimap; Survival starts WITHOUT it, shows "Owned" once bought), and **ONE MERGED weapon card per weapon (2026-07-17, `weaponItem(w)` — replaced the separate Buy + "Upgrade <Weapon>" cards)**: while UNOWNED the card (id = the weapon key, e.g. `'shotgun'`) sells the weapon itself (append to a free slot + full ammo; **buying while all 3 slots are full opens the REPLACE chooser** — `shopPurchase` returns `'choose-replace'`, then a click on `shopReplaceWeapon(oldW)` swaps the new weapon into that slot and deducts the score, `shopCancelReplace` aborts); once OWNED the SAME card turns into the upgrade seller (name gains the sold tier's roman numeral, e.g. "Shotgun II"), selling Level 2 then Level 3 (max → "Maxed" note): each level adds +`upgradeDamagePct` (**40% since 2026-08-13, user request — was 25%**) of the weapon's BASE damage (`player.weaponLvl` → `weaponDamage()` in weapons.js; the launcher's blast scales too), per-tier prices in `CFG.shop.upgradeCosts[w]`, levels reset each run and persist if a weapon is replaced away then re-bought. The PISTOL is never sold (starting loadout) — its card exists only while owned (upgrade variant), and disappears if the pistol is replaced away (matching the old upgrade-card gating). Buys are rejected (score kept) when full/owned/insufficient/maxed. Clicking **Start Next Wave** (or SPACE/Enter via scene `shopKey`) first shows an **"Are you ready? Yes / No"** confirm (`requestNextWave`/`confirmNext` in shop.js); confirming (click Yes, or a second SPACE) actually starts the wave. `input.js` keys off `activeScene.shopActive()`: while the shop is open it swallows all gameplay keys and, on the deliberate pointer-unlock, pauses WITHOUT showing the pause blocker (cursor freed for the menu); `startNextWave()` closes the shop then calls `requestLock()` to re-lock + resume. `monasHp`/`monasMaxHp` are scene-local (max re-seeded from `CFG.survival.monasMaxHp` on `enter()`, so Strengthen resets each run). Ammo drops refill only `player.owned` weapons (+`ammoPickup` each, cap `maxAmmo`). **Monas HP objective (priority target, reworked 2026-07-07):** every survival robot attacks Monas by default — per-frame `atkMonas = monasHp>0 && distToEye > aggroM × CAMP_M`, where the effective aggro radius `aggroM` is normally `CFG.survival.playerAggroMeters` (15 m → 105 units) but SHRINKS once a robot commits to Monas: on its FIRST bite of Monas the robot sets `z.monasCommitted` and rolls `z.monasLocked = Math.random() < CFG.survival.monasLockChance` (0.5) ONCE — committed-but-unlocked robots use the reduced `CFG.survival.monasLockAggroMeters` (5 m → 35 units, much harder to peel off), LOCKED robots use radius 0 (never chase the player, fully committed). It only chases the player when the player is within that (possibly shrunk) aggro radius, and returns to Monas once the player leaves it (no static `z.target` / `monasAttackerChance` anymore). Monas attackers return `{}` from `robotAI` so they never attack the player; **per class (2026-07-12): melee C gnaws the AABB edge (stop 6, `monasClawDamage` per bite), ranged B/A STOP at `0.95×z.range` and SHOOT Monas' center every `fireDelaySec`** — the bullet carries `monasDmg` (= `monasClawDamage`) and on hitting the Monas silhouette triggers the scene hook `enemyBulletHitMonas` → `damageMonas` (+ a spark puff); the commit/lock roll happens on the first SHOT too. **MONAS COLLAPSE cinematic (2026-07-13):** `damageMonas` reaching `monasHp ≤ 0` does NOT call `gameOver` immediately — it sets scene flag `monasFalling`, calls `startMonasCollapse()` (world.js), and shows "THE MONUMENT IS FALLING!"; further Monas damage is ignored. The obelisk was built with its **tower pieces (foot/obelisk/cup/flame/glow/light) parented into a hinge group `monasTop`** at `MONAS_HINGE` (x 6.5, y 8 = platform surface / base edge) while the base steps stay static, so the tower can **topple about its base edge**. `updateMonasCollapse(dt)` (driven each frame by `updateMode`, which skips the wave machine while `monasFalling`) runs a 3-phase animation from `CFG.survival.monasCollapse{Tremble,Topple,Settle}Sec`: TREMBLE (rising shake of `monasTop`, dust puffs from the base via `spawnGroundPuff`, flame dims, cam shake via renderer `addCamShake`), TOPPLE (gravity-accelerated `rotation.z` to ~−(π/2+0.14), dust dragged along the falling length, then a big IMPACT near flat = dust burst + marble **debris chunks** [shared box geo + the Monas stone/marble materials, gravity+spin+bounce] + heavy cam shake + flame extinguished), SETTLE (small bounce + dust settle); it returns `true` when done → `gameOver(false, 'THE MONUMENT HAS FALLEN')`. **While the tower falls ALL robots CELEBRATE** exactly as they do when the player dies — `updateRobots` gates `celebrateRobot` on `isPlayerDying() || activeScene.robotsCelebrate()`, and the survival scene's **`robotsCelebrate()` hook returns `monasFalling`** (so robots stop attacking, turn to the player, and cheer during the collapse). During the collapse the flame is taken off decor.js (`setFlameLight(null)`/`setFlameGlow(null)`) so it can be killed without the pulse overriding. `resetMonasCollapse()` (called in `enter()`, since the world persists across runs) stands the tower back up, restores the flame to decor, and disposes the debris/geo. **Scoring** (`CFG.robot.score`, applied in `killRobot(i, opts)`): class C kill 100 (`normalKill`), ranged class A/B kill 150 (`specialKill`) (boss = `bosses.giant.score` / tank `bosses.tank.score`) — no headshot bonus (headshots removed 2026-07-11); death spawns gore (see the GORE bullet above). Wave events = the **Monas-fog event ONLY** (the old random every-5-waves `scene.fog` fog + blackout events were REMOVED 2026-07-11 — only the `EVT` state machine + `applyLightPreset(scene,'outdoor')` on enter remain). **`'monasfog'` is a THICK grey POSITION-BASED fog covering the whole arena EXCEPT a clear circle of `CFG.survival.monasFogClearMeters` (15 m) around Monas** — impossible with camera-distance `scene.fog`, so it's a translucent grey **overhead canopy disk** (`world.js` `createFogCanopy` — built ONCE at world-build so preload's `renderer.compile` warms it, no hitch; a 1024² radial canvas texture with `destination-out` punching a soft transparent hole at the disk center = Monas at world origin) floating at y 26, faded by the `EVT` envelope via `setFogCanopy(k)`/`driftFogCanopy(dt)` (slow spin about Monas keeps the clear hole locked on Monas); `endEvent()` calls `setFogCanopy(0)`. **Trigger:** eligible from wave `monasFogFromWave` (4 = "after wave 3"); `startWave` rolls `chance = min(monasFogChanceMax (0.75), monasFogChanceBase (0.20) + monasFogChancePerWave (0.03)·(n − fromWave))` (capped at 75%, reached ~wave 22). Duration `monasFogDurationSec` (60 s). Damage direction indicator: one reused `#hitDir` wedge rotated by `attackerAngle()`; end-of-run stats (`stats` in state.js, per-bullet accuracy) fill `#goStats` on the game-over screen.
